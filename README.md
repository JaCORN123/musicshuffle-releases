# MusicShuffle

Shuffle your music, build playlists, and download songs from YouTube and SoundCloud as audio.

## Download

Get the newest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/JaCORN123/musicshuffle-releases/releases/latest)**:

- **Windows:** `MusicShuffle.exe` (download it and double-click; if Windows SmartScreen appears, choose *More info → Run anyway*)
- **Android:** `MusicShuffle.apk` (open it on your phone and allow *Install unknown apps* when asked)

Both apps update themselves when a new version comes out.

## Moving between devices

In either app choose **Export all data**, move the file to your other device, then choose **Import data** there.

## RECCOMENDED SETUP:

It is heavily reccomended that you begin on Windows with MusicShuffle.exe. 
On launch, go to the "Discover" tab, and press "Sign in", next to the search button. 

Make sure Firefox is installed; ensure you are logged into Firefox, and logged into a Google Account that can also access YouTube. 

Press the "Use the account signed in to my browser", then select Firefox. 
Then press save. 

I personally reccomend to make all changes on Desktop; to transfer to your mobile device, simply export all your data, upload it to a service like Google Drive, then upload it onto your phone on the apk file.

## IMPORTING PLAYLISTS

The CSV file import is the best way to import a playlist.
Use a service like Exportify to export your Spotify playlists to a CSV file. 

Then, press "Import CSV" on the Discover page. 
All of your songs will be automatically imported. You will be made aware of any errors that occur. 

## UPDATES:

On Desktop, a popup will occur when an update is available. 

For Mobile, navigate to Settings, scroll all the way to the bottom under "Updates", and press "Check for updates". 

## DOWNLOAD ISSUES

If you make most of your changes on mobile, ensure your Downloader is made up to date frequently, as services often change.
Simply navigate to the "Settings" tab, and press "Update downloader".
